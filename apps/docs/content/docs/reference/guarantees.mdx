description: The honest identity, voting, and Comments guarantees of the Notion-only Profile.
---

## What Feedbax provides

- A browser-held Device Profile for continuity on one browser.
- Best-effort duplicate-vote resistance within that profile.
- Public Comments with displayed participant names.
- Server validation and configurable mutation rate limits.

## What it does not provide

- Verified human identity.
- Cross-device account continuity.
- One-person-one-vote enforcement.
- Proof that a displayed Comment author is who they claim to be.

Verified identity, reliable unique voting, following, notifications, and Participant media belong to the planned Connected Profile. They are outside the supported 0.2.0 Notion-only Profile.
