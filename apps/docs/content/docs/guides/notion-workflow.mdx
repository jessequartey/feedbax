## Recommended lifecycle

1. New submissions arrive with their public fields and stable Feedbax ID.
2. Product Team members classify, merge, and prioritize them in Notion.
3. A public status moves from Planned to In Progress to Shipped only when the promise is real.
4. Shipped work can link to a Changelog entry.

Private planning fields should never be mapped to public content fields. Keep sensitive research and participant details outside the public schema.
