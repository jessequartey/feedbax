---
title: Notion workflow
description: Triage and publish Posts from the Product Team's Notion Workspace.
---

1. New submissions arrive as New, unpublished Posts. Review the Inbox view.
2. Use Reviewing and Prioritization to investigate and plan. Owner, Priority, Strategic Theme, Participant Segment, Impact, Effort, Decision Note, and Needs Follow-up are private planning fields.
3. Set Published when a Post is suitable for public display. Publication is independent of Status.
4. Move work through Reviewing, Planned, In Progress, Shipped, or Closed. The public roadmap shows Planned, In Progress, and Shipped.
5. Add a public Team Response and an optional root-relative or HTTPS Release Notes URL. Keep sensitive information in private properties or the page body.
6. Write Product Team Comments and replies in Notion. Resolve or delete a discussion in Notion to remove it from the public discussion after refresh.

The six generated views can be customized in Notion. Diagnostics report missing default views and private workflow properties without changing them. Do not delete or change the type of a required runtime property; renamed properties continue working through their stable IDs.

Direct Notion edits may take up to two minutes to appear in public cached Post projections. Comments use a separate read path without a long-lived shared cache.
