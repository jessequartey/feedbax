title: Comments and voting
description: Understand Participation behavior and moderate public discussion.
---

Votes and Comments help Product Teams understand interest and context; they are not verified identities or scientific polling.

## Moderation

- Remove sensitive information from public Comments.
- Treat vote counts as directional signals.
- Keep Team Responses factual and link to shipped work when possible.
- Document stronger identity requirements outside the Notion-only Profile.

See [Participation guarantees](/docs/reference/guarantees) for the exact boundary.
