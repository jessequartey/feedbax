Feedbax is a self-hosted feedback portal for Product Teams that use Notion as their working system.

## The model

```text
Participant → public Feedbax portal → Notion database → Product Team
```

The portal handles public reading, submission, Votes, Comments, Roadmap, and Changelog. Notion remains the source of truth for triage and publishing.

## Core 0.2 boundaries

- One Notion workspace and one feedback database.
- Notion-only Participation, with browser-scoped identity.
- Cloudflare Worker deployment.
- No Feedbax-hosted identity, billing, or analytics service.

Read [Participation guarantees](/docs/reference/guarantees) before promising identity or voting properties to Participants.
