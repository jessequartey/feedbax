description: Directional scope for the Notion MVP and what follows it.
---

A feature is not shipped until it appears in a tagged release.

## v0.0.2 — Notion MVP

**Foundation only:** connector-oriented domain/configuration boundaries, authentication interfaces, application shells, deployment presets, and cross-provider runtime evidence.

**Planned for v0.0.2:** Notion schema validation, submissions, search, filters, duplicate suggestions, unique voting, comments, post details, public roadmap and changelog, signed identity sessions, branding, actionable errors, and CDN-friendly caching.

## After the MVP

**Deferred:** embedded widgets, status notifications, RSS and digests, advanced triage, importing/exporting, customer context, Netlify support, and a project generator.

## Ecosystem direction

GitHub, Linear, Google Sheets, SQLite/Postgres, generic API connectors, and public extension contracts may follow only after the Notion workflow proves the model.
