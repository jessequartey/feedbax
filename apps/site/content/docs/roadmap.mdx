A feature is not shipped until it appears in a tagged release.

## v0.1.0 — generator-led Notion preview

**Preview available:** generator-led installation, Notion schema validation, submissions, search, filters, duplicate suggestions, best-effort voting, comments, post details, public roadmap and changelog, three identity modes, branding, actionable errors, CDN-friendly caching, and three deployment targets.

**Release prerequisites:** npm ownership, the live Notion credential smoke, and an independent quickstart remain before publication.

## After the MVP

**Deferred:** embedded widgets, status notifications, RSS and digests, advanced triage, importing/exporting, customer context, and Netlify support.

## Ecosystem direction

GitHub, Linear, Google Sheets, Better Auth, SQLite/Postgres, and generic API connectors may follow only after the Notion workflow proves the model.
