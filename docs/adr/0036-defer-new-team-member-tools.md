# Defer new Team Member tools

The owner deferred new Team Member tools during 0.2.2 requirements review. Retain native Notion/GitHub workflows without adding a Feedbax Team Member login, privileged runtime endpoints, operation pages, or new Team Member CLI/bot commands. A new authorization system would expand the product and must not be improvised from Participant identity or the trusted-submission key.

Merges, private report review, verified restrictions, batch moderation, and manual recovery are delivered only where a safe native provider action meets their acceptance criteria; otherwise their execution remains explicitly deferred. Deployer-owned setup, read-only diagnostics, upgrade, backup/restore, and migration remain distinct lifecycle work. When restrictions are implemented, preserve prior Votes until separately moderated; legacy merged counts remain explicitly unverified and separate from unique Votes. Release notes must distinguish the amended scope from the original full handoff.
