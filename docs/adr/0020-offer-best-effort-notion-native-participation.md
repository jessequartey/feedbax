# Offer best-effort Notion-native participation

Feedbax keeps two capability profiles but moves explicitly best-effort voting and native Comments into the Notion-only Profile. Browser-remembered Votes and short-lived Comment Capabilities provide useful participation without claiming verified identity, one-person-one-vote integrity, durable attribution, or reliable cross-device social state. The Connected Profile remains responsible for verified Participant identity, reliable unique voting, following, notifications, signed identity handoff, abuse-resistant social state, and Participant media storage.
