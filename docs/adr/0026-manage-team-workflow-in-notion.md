# Keep Team workflow in Notion

[ADR-0034](0034-select-one-authoritative-content-provider.md) extends native Team workflow to the selected Content Provider. [ADR-0036](0036-defer-new-team-member-tools.md) preserves the absence of new Team Member tools.

Owner, prioritization, segmentation, impact, effort, decisions, and follow-up are managed through private Notion properties and views rather than a separate portal dashboard. Runtime properties are strict; changes to private workflow fields produce diagnostics but do not stop the public portal.
