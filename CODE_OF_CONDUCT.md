# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Feedbax welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes rather than people.
- Respect project scope, review decisions, privacy, and security boundaries.
- Accept correction, take responsibility for mistakes, and help repair harm.
- Keep private information, credentials, and vulnerability details out of public discussions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers interpret and enforce this code in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban participation according to the severity and pattern of behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. For security-sensitive reports, use the process in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be handled with appropriate confidentiality, and retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

Maintainers who have a conflict of interest in a report must recuse themselves when another maintainer is available.
